## Overview

Advanced study of nonlinear dynamical systems with applications to physics, biology, and engineering. Topics include phase portraits, bifurcation theory, chaos, and stability analysis.

## Sample notation

A continuous-time system $\dot{x} = f(x)$ has an equilibrium at $x^*$ when $f(x^*) = 0$. Local stability is read from the Jacobian $Df(x^*)$: if all eigenvalues satisfy $\mathrm{Re}(\lambda) < 0$, the equilibrium is asymptotically stable.

A one-parameter family can undergo a Hopf bifurcation when a complex conjugate pair crosses the imaginary axis:

$$
\lambda(\mu) = \alpha(\mu) \pm i\omega(\mu), \qquad \alpha(0) = 0,\ \omega(0) > 0,\ \alpha'(0) \neq 0.
$$
