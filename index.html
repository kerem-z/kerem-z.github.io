About.

Hi there! I'm Kerem Zengin, a Master's student and Research Assistant at the Institute of Applied Mathematics, METU. I focus on Explainable AI (XAI), causality, and embedding techniques, particularly in understanding how information is structured and interpreted in machine learning models.

I take a computationalist approach, emphasizing formal structures and abstraction over emergent behavior. Influenced by Wittgenstein's philosophy, I am more interested in the fundamental problems underlying AI and representation rather than domain-specific challenges. My work explores how knowledge is structured, how classification emerges, and what constraints shape machine understanding.
