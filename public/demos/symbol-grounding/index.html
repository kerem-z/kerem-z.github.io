The symbol and the apple

Kerem Zengin · 4 November 2026

Draft. A demo for the Symbol Grounding post. The writing is a sketch. The two widgets are the part to keep.

TL;DR

A symbol can spend its whole life pointing at other symbols. Apple means fruit, fruit means food, and somewhere the list comes back. Nothing in that circle is an apple you could hold.

Grounding is a second link: the same word also sits next to the thing, the way the sketch on the blog does. The circle of words can stay. One line leaves it.

Follow a word

Pick a word. In Symbols only, each one hands you another phrase. In Grounded, apple also shows an apple. The other three stay phrases. Grounding here is not a picture for every word. It is one exit.

Open the circle

Four words name each other. Press the button and the apple node grows a second link, down to a small apple. The circle does not disappear. A grounded symbol can still mean fruit. It just does not mean only that.

Where this sits

The sketch on the blog index is the same idea in one drawing: a mark, a dashed arrow, an apple. The other drafts on that list are the Chinese room, a sentence that talks about itself, and Mary’s black-and-white room. They are neighbors of this one, not chapters of it.
