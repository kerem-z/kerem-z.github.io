Research Interests

Research Focus

The research focuses on formal approaches to Explainable AI (XAI), causality, and embedding techniques, investigating how information is represented, structured, and processed in machine learning systems. The work emphasizes mathematical frameworks for improving model interpretability.

Key areas include meta-learning, representation learning, and ontology, with particular emphasis on knowledge formation and classification. The approach is grounded in computationalism, prioritizing formal abstraction over emergent behavior. Current investigations explore meaning construction in AI systems, learning constraints, and structured representations through type theory and hypergraphs.

Current Projects

Causal Frameworks for Explainable AI

Investigating the intersection of causality and model interpretability through formal methods. This project aims to develop rigorous frameworks for understanding how deep learning models process information and make decisions, using causal structures and embedding techniques to bridge the gap between statistical patterns and meaningful explanations.

Mathematical Foundations of Knowledge Representation

Exploring the mathematical structures underlying knowledge representation in AI systems through category theory and type theory. This theoretical project focuses on developing formal abstractions for learning processes, investigating how different mathematical frameworks can capture the essence of knowledge formation and transformation in artificial systems.
